The Fami Advantage

Why Landlords, Courts & Property Managers Trust Fami Construction Ltd

We solve the everyday headaches faced by Kenyan property owners—from unvetted relief guards and absentee cleaners to unmaintained generators and delayed tenant repairs.

01

Zero Reliever Gaps

When a cleaner or security guard is on leave or unwell, our Westlands rapid-deployment pool dispatches a briefed, uniformed reliever before shift handover—so your property is never left understaffed.

02

Service-Charge Transparency

Estate Boards of Management (BoMs) and managing agents receive clear monthly KPI scorecards, consumable logs, diesel generator runtime sheets, and consolidated invoicing with zero hidden markups.

03

Kenyan Statutory Compliance

All deployed personnel are engaged in full compliance with Kenyan labour laws (NSSF, SHIF/NHIF, Housing Levy, WIBA insurance) and vetted with valid DCI Certificates of Good Conduct—shielding landlords from vicarious liability.

04

Water & Power Resilience

In Nairobi, tenant satisfaction hinges on uninterrupted water and power. We proactively maintain borehole pumps, float switches, underground/rooftop tanks, and standby generators before outages strike.

05

In-House Construction Capability

Unlike pure cleaning agencies, we are builders at heart. When your property needs cabro repair, roof waterproofing, perimeter wall reinforcement, or an office fit-out, our civil & engineering crew executes it directly.

06

Diaspora & Institutional Reporting

Own property in Nairobi while living abroad? We provide photo-verified inspection reports, preventative maintenance schedules, and direct executive oversight by our Westlands leadership team.
